# Scene 10 — MyNetWorth End Card

Work in `~/mynetworth-videos`.

Create the final branded end card for the MyNetWorth promotional video using **Remotion**. Do not use Kling or any generative video tools.

## Specifications

- Duration: 5 seconds
- Resolution: 1920 × 1080
- Frame rate: Match the existing production
- Output: H.264 MP4
- Dialogue: None

## Design

Create a clean, elegant, professional end card with a dark navy background, consistent with the visual identity of MyNetWorth.

Use the actual MyNetWorth logo from the project if available. Do not invent or redesign the logo.

Display the following text:

**MyNetWorth**

**All Your Investments. One Clean Dashboard.**

**Available on Android**

**mynetworth.in**

**Download link in description**

### Visual hierarchy

1. MyNetWorth — large, prominent, centered.
2. Tagline — immediately beneath the product name, smaller and understated.
3. Available on Android — clearly visible.
4. mynetworth.in — prominent and easy to remember.
5. Download link in description — small, secondary text.

Use generous spacing, clean typography and strong contrast.

Keep the layout visually balanced and readable on mobile screens.

Do not include a QR code, iOS references, pricing or additional marketing claims.

## Animation

- Transition gently from the final frame of Scene 9 into the end card.
- Fade in the end-card content over approximately 0.5 seconds.
- Keep all elements stationary after appearing.
- Hold the complete end card for at least 4 seconds.
- No zooms, bouncing text, particles, moving backgrounds or distracting effects.
- No additional dialogue or sound effects.

## Integration

Add Scene 10 after Scene 9 in the existing Remotion production composition.

Preserve Scenes 1–9 exactly as they are.

Ensure the transition from Scene 9 is smooth and that the final end card remains visible until the video ends.

Do not introduce a black frame or unintended gap between scenes.

## Deliverables

1. Standalone Scene 10 video at 1080p.
2. Complete Scenes 1–10 preview at 1080p.
3. A still image of the final end card for review.
4. Report the total duration of the assembled film.

Save outputs under the existing production output structure.

Do not overwrite previous approved renders.

**Stop after rendering for review. Do not make unrelated changes to the project.**
