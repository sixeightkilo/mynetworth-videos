## Dialogue

Vijay looks toward Nikhil and says:

**VIJAY:**
"Looks good. But I've tried these finance apps before. You give them your details, and suddenly every loan company in the country has your number!"

His delivery is natural, mildly skeptical, with a touch of dry humor on the final phrase.

Nikhil listens without interrupting.

He then replies casually:

**NIKHIL:**
"Not MyNetWorth. Your data never leaves your phone."

Pronounce MyNetWorth naturally as "My Net Worth."

Nikhil's delivery is straightforward and reassuring, without exaggerated emphasis or a triumphant smile.

Vijay listens and gives a small, natural expression of acknowledgement.

No additional dialogue.
